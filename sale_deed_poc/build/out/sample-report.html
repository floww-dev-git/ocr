How this report was produced — and its limits

Reading (AI): each deed image/PDF was read by a vision model into a structured record. Judgement (code): the chain verdict is computed by deterministic rules (identity, property, recital and date continuity) — not by the model — so it is explainable and repeatable.

Not certified here: legal marketability of title, encumbrances/litigation not in the bundle, and authenticity against the government registry. India has no official title-lookup API; registry cross-check (Encumbrance Certificate, e-stamp / QR / digital-signature verification) is a planned Phase-2 advisory step. Until then this is a screening aid that flags for a human, never an auto-clearance.
